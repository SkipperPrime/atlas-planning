## Evidence gaps and qualifications

- ATLAS-001–003 and several early slices are reconstructed from ADR-0011 lineage; their detailed
  contemporaneous acceptance checkpoints are not consistently represented in the current audit set.
- ATLAS-008, 009, 012–014, 017, 029B, and 034 have incomplete or distributed overall closeout
  evidence. Their records above state only what the extant slices, ADRs, or audits establish.
- ATLAS-016 deferred a fresh Aria harvest; ATLAS-027 explicitly awaited human acceptance;
  ATLAS-031E and ATLAS-032B retain human review/deferred scope.
- ATLAS-018 through 024 include research outcomes that range from experiment closure and failed
  grounding to qualified product proof. These outcomes are not recast as unqualified product
  acceptance.
- A branch, base commit, or closeout-document commit is not represented as an accepted checkpoint
  unless its source identifies it as such. Full SHA resolution is intentionally left for a later
  evidence reconciliation rather than guessed.

The implementation repo’s audit files, ADRs, and Git history are the source of truth. This index
should be corrected when additional primary evidence is found, without rewriting the underlying
history.
